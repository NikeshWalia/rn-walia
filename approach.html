Approach

Understanding first. Advice second.

How an engagement usually unfolds, how to prepare for a first conversation, and answers to common questions.

Principle

Two plots of the same size can call for very different buildings. Two similar houses on the same road can differ in value. Useful advice depends on noticing those differences.

That is why the work begins by listening and by looking at the site, the building, the documents and the purpose behind the enquiry, before any recommendation is made.

Process

  1. Initial enquiry

    You get in touch and outline your requirement. The enquiry builder on this website can help you organise the essentials (the property, its location and the purpose) before you share them.

  2. Understanding the requirement

    A conversation to understand what you need and why. This is also when the appropriate kind of help is identified, and when you can ask about scope and fees.

  3. Reviewing relevant information

    Documents, drawings and other details relevant to the requirement are reviewed. You will be told what is needed and why; nothing more than necessary is requested.

  4. Professional consultation or assessment

    The professional work itself: design consultation, site inspection or assessment, depending on the service.

  5. Discussing the next steps

    Findings, options and any limitations are explained plainly, together with what would happen next.

A general outline, not a fixed procedure or a guarantee. The steps, timing and documents involved depend on the service and the requirement.

Preparation

You don't need everything in order before getting in touch. These details make the first conversation far more useful.

Helpful to have ready

  • A short description of what you need, and why
  • The general location of the property: sector, village or town
  • The type of property: house, flat, plot, shop or other
  • Any plot plan, drawings or approved building plan you have
  • Photographs, if the property is far away or hard to access
  • For valuations: the purpose, and any format required by a bank or institution
  • Any timeline you are working to

What this website does not do

It does not estimate property values, produce valuation reports, give legal conclusions, or assess structural safety. Those require professional engagement with the property and its documents.

The enquiry builder only organises your information into a clear summary. The screen always tells you plainly whether anything has been sent.

Questions

Can you tell me what my property is worth over the phone, or from this website?

No. A valuation is a professional opinion that depends on the property's documents, location, construction and condition, and on the purpose of the valuation. It cannot responsibly be given without a proper assessment. This website does not estimate property values.

What should I bring to a first meeting?

Whatever you already have that describes the property, such as a plot or site plan, existing or approved drawings, ownership documents and photographs, along with a note of what you need. If something is missing, that is fine; the office can tell you what is needed.

Which areas do you serve?

The office is in Panchkula and serves Panchkula, Kalka, Moginand and surrounding areas. If your property is elsewhere, mention its location in your enquiry.

Is the practice part of the District Court?

No. The office is located within the District Court Complex in Sector 1, Panchkula, but R.N. Walia is an independent professional practice. It is not part of, or affiliated with, the court or any government department.

Will my bank accept the valuation?

Banks and other institutions set their own requirements, and some accept valuations only from valuers on their own panel. Please check with your bank or institution before commissioning a valuation for their use.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the service, the property and the scope of work, so they are discussed once the requirement is understood. Ask about fees at the start; it is a sensible question for any professional engagement.

Does the online enquiry builder send my details automatically?

No. It prepares an organised summary in your browser. Nothing is sent until you choose to share it yourself, for example by opening it in WhatsApp or your email app, or by bringing it to the office.

How do I find the office?

The office is at the District Court Complex, Sector 1, Panchkula. As a landmark, look for the Old Lawyers' Chambers; the office is listed opposite Chamber No. 6. Open directions in Google Maps.
