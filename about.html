About

R.N. Walia, Architect & Valuer.

An architectural and valuation practice in Panchkula, Haryana.

The practice

Two closely related disciplines, one way of working.

R.N. Walia is an architect and valuer whose practice is based at the District Court Complex in Sector 1, Panchkula, serving clients in Panchkula, Kalka, Moginand and the surrounding areas.

Architecture is concerned with how buildings are planned, designed and made. Valuation is concerned with understanding what a property is, and what it is worth for a particular purpose. Both depend on the same habits: looking carefully, reading documents properly, and explaining conclusions clearly.

Careful assessment, clear explanation and proper documentation, whatever the size of the matter.
