    Process

    Understanding first, advice second. Most engagements follow a similar path.

    1. Initial enquiry

      You get in touch by phone, through the enquiry builder or with a visit to the office, and outline what you need.

    2. Understanding the requirement

      A conversation about the property, its purpose and the kind of help that is appropriate.

    3. Reviewing information

      Relevant documents, drawings or details are reviewed, so advice rests on facts.

    4. Consultation or assessment

      The professional work itself: design consultation, inspection or assessment, as required.

    5. Next steps

      Findings and options are explained plainly, along with what would happen next.

    A general outline, not a fixed procedure. The exact steps, timing and documents depend on the service and on each requirement.
